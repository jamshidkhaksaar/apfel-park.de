## 2025-04-03 - Accessible Notification Badges
**Learning:** Hardcoded numeric notification badges (e.g., "1") lack context for screen readers, leading to confusing announcements (just the number).
**Action:** Always replace uncontextualized numbers in notification badges with screen-reader-friendly HTML by hiding the visual number (`aria-hidden="true"`) and adding descriptive, context-aware, screen-reader-only text (e.g., `className="sr-only"`).
